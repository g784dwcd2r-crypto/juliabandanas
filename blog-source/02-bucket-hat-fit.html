Hats are the largest part of most seasons and the thing most often ordered in the wrong size. It is an easy mistake to make, because bucket hats are sized in a way that assumes you already know how they are sized.

Here is the whole thing, in about two minutes.

Measure once, properly

Take a soft tape measure and run it around your head just above the ears and across the middle of the forehead — roughly where the hat band will actually sit. Keep it level. Snug, not tight; you should be able to slide a finger under it.

No tape measure? Use a length of string or a phone charging cable, mark where it meets, then measure that against a ruler.

Write the number down in centimetres. That number, not your instinct, is your size.

Most of the bucket hats in a season are a 56–58cm one size, which genuinely does fit a wide range of heads. It is not a euphemism for “medium”. Where a product has several sizes, they are listed on its own page and the listing measurement is the one to trust — not the size letter.

The three mistakes

Measuring over hair rather than around the head. A lot of hair adds a centimetre or two, which is real and should be counted. But measuring over a bun or a ponytail is not, and it will send you a size too big.

Treating “one size” as a warning. On bucket hats it is usually accurate. The unstructured crown has more give than a fitted cap, and the brim does not pull.

Sizing up “to be safe”. A hat that is slightly too small is uncomfortable. A hat that is too big slides down over your eyebrows in any wind at all, which is worse and considerably funnier to everyone else.

Bucket hat, cap, or reversible

Three different fits, and they are not interchangeable.

Bucket hats sit down on the head and hold their position by contact with the whole band. Get the measurement right and they stay put. They pack flat, they survive being stuffed in a bag, and the brim gives real shade.

Caps are usually adjustable — a snap or a slider at the back — so the measurement matters much less. If you are between sizes anywhere, a cap is the safer purchase.

Reversible hats are two hats in one and fit fractionally tighter than a single-layer equivalent, because there are two layers of fabric in the band. If you are at the very top of a size range, bear that in mind.

How it should look on

The band sits just above the ears, level, with the brim clear of your eyebrows. You should be able to shake your head briskly and have it stay on, without it having left a red line across your forehead when you take it off.

If it lifts when you look up, it is too big. If it leaves a mark, it is too small.
